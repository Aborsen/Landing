## The account menu

Everything about your account lives in one place: click your email at the bottom of the left sidebar to open the account menu. From there you can reach:

- **My Account** — your email, password, theme, and account deletion.
- **Manage Plan** — change the plan you are on.
- **Billing** — payment details and invoices.
- **Balance** — how many tokens you have left.
- **Support** — leave feedback, or open the resources.
- **Log Out**.

Plan and billing details are covered in [Payments & Billing](/docs/payments-billing), and how usage is counted in [Plans and tokens](/docs/plans-and-tokens).

## Your email and password

Open **My Account** to see the email your account uses and to **Change password**. If you signed up with Google, access is controlled by your Google account and there is no Insightis password to change — see [Security](/docs/security).

## Choosing a theme

Insightis ships in light and dark. Under **Theme** in **My Account**, pick **Light**, **Dark**, or **System** to follow your operating system's setting.

## Checking your usage

The usage meter above your email in the sidebar shows how much of your monthly allowance you have used — for example *673 / 1000*. **Balance** in the account menu shows the same figure with more detail. When you run low you can top up or move to a larger plan; see [Plans and tokens](/docs/plans-and-tokens).

## Deleting your account

**My Account → Delete account** removes your Insightis account permanently. This also clears the datasets you have connected or uploaded and your chat sessions, so export anything you want to keep first.

> [!WARNING]
> Deletion cannot be undone. If you only want to stop paying, change your plan from **Manage Plan** instead of deleting the account.

## Getting help

Use **Support → Leave Feedback** in the account menu to send us a note, or [contact us](/company/contacts) directly. For questions about how something works, the rest of these [docs](/docs/) and the [Prompt Library](/resources/prompt-library) are the fastest places to look.
