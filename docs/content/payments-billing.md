## Where billing lives

Billing is in the account menu at the bottom of the left sidebar:

- **Manage Plan** — see the plan you are on and switch to another one.
- **Billing** — your payment details and invoices.
- **Balance** — the tokens you have left this cycle.

Current plans and prices are always on the [Pricing page](/pricing).

## Payment methods

Insightis accepts major credit and debit cards, processed by Stripe. Card details are handled by Stripe and are not stored on Insightis servers — only the last four digits and the card brand are kept so you can recognise the card on file.

## Buying more tokens

If you use up your monthly allowance before the cycle ends, you can buy a one-time token pack rather than waiting for the refill or moving up a plan. One-time tokens do not expire, and Insightis only draws on them once your monthly allowance is spent.

See [Plans and tokens](/docs/plans-and-tokens) for how the two kinds of tokens work together.

## Changing or cancelling your plan

Use **Manage Plan** to move between plans. Changing plans keeps your workspace intact — your metric definitions, connections, and files stay exactly as they are.

If you cancel, your workspace stays available on the Free plan; you do not lose what you have set up. To remove your data as well, delete the account instead — see [Managing your account](/docs/managing-your-account).

## Help with billing

If something looks wrong on an invoice — a duplicate charge, an unexpected amount, or a refund request — email **billing@insightis.ai** with the invoice number and a short description, or [contact us](/company/contacts). Most billing questions are resolved within one business day.
